# Refund Policy

**Last updated:** October 24, 2026

This Refund Policy explains how refunds work for the Haiku generation service. We try to be fair while keeping things sustainable for a small, low-cost product.

**Billing is processed by Paddle**, our payment provider and **merchant of record**. Paddle collects payment, applies taxes where applicable, and handles checkout. Some refund steps may go through Paddle’s systems or policies; we work within that framework.

---

## 1. General approach

Digital credits are easy to use right away. Because of that, **we generally do not offer refunds** once you have **used** the Haiku generation service in a way that consumed what you paid for—for example, after you have actively used paid features or redeemed credits.

This isn’t meant to be harsh; it reflects that the product is inexpensive, run by a solo developer, and costs real time and infrastructure to operate. We still want to fix real mistakes and genuine problems (see below).

---

## 2. When we may refund or credit

We may offer a **refund, partial refund, or account credit** when something is clearly wrong on our side or with billing, such as:

- **Duplicate charge** for the same product or period  
- **Technical failure** that prevented you from using what you paid for for a meaningful period, and we could not reasonably fix it in time  
- **Billing error** (for example, you were charged the wrong amount)  
- **Other exceptional cases** we judge in good faith—usually rare, and decided case by case  

We **do not** guarantee a refund in every situation. Each request is reviewed on its facts.

---

## 3. No subscriptions

The Haiku generation service currently sells **one-time credit packs only** (100 haiku generations for **USD 3.00**, plus applicable tax). There are **no subscriptions or recurring charges**, so there is nothing to cancel and you will never be billed again unless you choose to buy another pack.

---

## 4. One-time credit purchases

Credits are **consumed when you use** features that spend them. **Purchased credits are not refundable** after they have been used, except where section 2 applies (e.g. duplicate charge or a serious technical issue that blocked use).

If credits were **never usable** due to a fault on our side and we confirm that, we will work with you on a reasonable fix, which may include restoring credits or a refund where appropriate.

---

## 5. How to request a refund or billing help

1. **Contact us** at [support@gpderp.party](mailto:support@gpderp.party). Include your account email, approximate date of charge, and a short description of the issue.  
2. If Paddle sent you a receipt or customer portal link, you can also **check Paddle’s buyer support** for payment-specific questions; as merchant of record, Paddle may handle certain requests directly.  

We aim to reply within a **reasonable time**. Complex cases may take a bit longer. If we approve a refund, it is usually processed back through the original payment method, subject to Paddle’s and your bank’s processing times.

---

## 6. Chargebacks

If you have a problem, please **contact us first** so we can try to resolve it. Unnecessary chargebacks can lead to account restrictions and make it harder to help you quickly.

---

## 7. Changes

We may update this Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of paid features after updates means you accept the revised Policy for new purchases.

---

## 8. Contact

For refund or billing questions, email [support@gpderp.party](mailto:support@gpderp.party). We will do our best to be helpful and clear.
