# Acceptable Use Policy

**Last updated:** October 24, 2026

This Acceptable Use Policy (“AUP”) sets out rules for using the Haiku generation service. It is part of how we keep the product workable for everyone. The [Terms of Service](/terms) also apply, including for enforcement.

---

## Allowed use

Use the Haiku generation service only for lawful purposes and in line with our Terms. Personal, hobby, and reasonable commercial use are fine unless we say otherwise for a specific plan or feature.

---

## Prohibited conduct

You must not:

- Break the law or violate anyone else’s rights  
- Try to access accounts, data, or systems you are not authorized to use  
- Disrupt or overload the Haiku generation service (including malware, aggressive scraping, or attempts to bypass limits or security)  
- Reverse engineer or misuse the Haiku generation service beyond what the law allows and we cannot contract out of  
- Use the Haiku generation service to create or spread unlawful, harmful, abusive, or harassing content  
- Impersonate others or misrepresent your affiliation  
- Use the Haiku generation service to send spam or unsolicited bulk communications through our features  

---

## Enforcement

We may warn you, suspend access, or close accounts if we reasonably believe you have breached this AUP or the Terms, or if you create risk for the Haiku generation service or other people. We try to be fair and proportionate; serious or repeated issues may lead to immediate termination.

---

## Reporting

If you see abuse tied to the Haiku generation service, email [support@gpderp.party](mailto:support@gpderp.party). We cannot promise a specific outcome for every report, but we review genuine issues.

---

## Changes

We may update this AUP from time to time. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use after updates means you accept the revised AUP for ongoing use.

---

## Contact

Questions about this policy: email [support@gpderp.party](mailto:support@gpderp.party).
