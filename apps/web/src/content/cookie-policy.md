# Cookie Policy

**Last updated:** October 24, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how the Haiku generation service uses cookies and similar technologies. It is a short companion to our [Privacy Policy](/privacy), which has more detail on personal data overall.

---

## What cookies and similar technologies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We also use similar technologies where relevant, such as **local storage** for preferences or session-related data in the browser.

---

## How we use them

**Essential / functional.** We use cookies or storage as needed to run the Haiku generation service—for example, to keep you signed in, protect against abuse, remember UI choices, or route traffic securely. These are not used for advertising on our site.

**Analytics.** We may use limited, privacy-conscious analytics to understand aggregate usage (such as which pages or features are used). We aim to keep this proportionate to operating a small product, not to build invasive profiles.

**Payments.** When you pay, **Paddle** (our merchant of record) operates checkout. Paddle may set their own cookies or use similar technologies on their checkout pages or portals, under [Paddle’s privacy notice](https://www.paddle.com/legal/privacy) and the terms shown at purchase.

---

## Your choices

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking or deleting **essential** cookies may mean some features stop working—for example, you may need to sign in again more often.

For analytics, if we offer a simple opt-out or use a provider with industry opt-out tools, we will describe that in the app or update this page when relevant.

---

## Updates

We may update this Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do.
